Questions

Fiberglass Resurfacing FAQ

Can fiberglass go over my plaster, concrete or vinyl liner pool?

Yes. Armor Fiberglass resurfaces many kinds of existing pools and spas with fiberglass, including plaster, concrete, vinyl liner and older fiberglass pools. David looks at your current surface during the free in-home estimate and explains what the job involves.

How long does a fiberglass pool finish last?

Armor Fiberglass expects its fiberglass finish to last at least 10 to 12 years, and up to 15 to 20 years. Results depend on care and water chemistry. The work also carries a 5-year warranty and a satisfaction guarantee.

Why choose fiberglass instead of replastering?

Fiberglass gives a smooth surface that resists stains and algae, and it can lower chemical costs over time. It is also one of the longest-lasting pool finishes available.

My pool is green. Can it still be resurfaced?

Yes. Armor Fiberglass does green pool clean-outs. Several of the before photos on this page show pools that started out green and were resurfaced in fiberglass.

Do you repair cracked pool slides?

Yes. Armor Fiberglass repairs and resurfaces factory and custom fiberglass and gel-coated slides, for a fraction of the cost of replacing the slide.
